# Section 508

Applies to US federal agencies when they develop, buy, maintain or use ICT, and in practice to vendors selling ICT to them (procurement requirement, often with an Accessibility Conformance Report based on the VPAT template).

## Standard

- Revised standards ("508 refresh") in effect since 18 January 2018.
- E205.4 and E207.2: electronic content and software must conform to WCAG 2.0 level A and AA. Chapter 3 functional performance criteria, Chapter 5 software, Chapter 6 support documentation and services.

## How the skill uses it

- `applies` only when the organisation is a US federal agency or sells ICT to one.
- Only criteria from WCAG 2.0 map to S508. 2.1 and 2.2 criteria stay WCAG22-only.
- When asked, the report can include a VPAT-style table (criterion, conformance level: supports, partially supports, does not support, not applicable, remarks). Hamu builds it from its own findings and never copies the ITI template text.
