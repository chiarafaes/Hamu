# Local search

Applies when the business serves people at or from a location (shop, studio, practice, service area).

## Google Business Profile (SEO.LOCAL.GBP)

- Verified profile with the business's real-world name (no keyword stuffing in the name, against the guidelines).
- Primary category that matches the main service, a few relevant secondary categories.
- Address or service area, opening hours including holidays, phone, website with UTM tagging.
- Photos of the place, team and work. Services or products listed.
- Reviews answered.

## Consistency (SEO.LOCAL.NAP)

Same name, address and phone on:
- the website (footer and contact page) and `LocalBusiness` schema,
- Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect,
- sector platforms and Belgian directories that matter in the sector, for example goudengids.be / pagesdor.be, local municipality or trade association listings, and sector review sites.

## Belgium specifics

- Bilingual or trilingual markets: profiles and location pages in the language of the area (Dutch in Flanders, French in Wallonia, both in Brussels, German in the East Cantons).
- Address format: street and number, postal code, municipality. Use the official municipality name in the local language.
- The enterprise number on the site supports trust, and is also an SP legal check.

## Location pages (SEO.LOCAL.PAGES)

One page per real location. City pages for places without a location are doorway pages unless they have genuinely local, useful content (projects done there, local specifics). See the rubric in `content-rubric.md`.
