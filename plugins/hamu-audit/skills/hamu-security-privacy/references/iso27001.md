# ISO/IEC 27001 readiness

Readiness only, status `readiness_only`. The skill never says an organisation is certified or compliant. Certification needs an accredited audit of the whole management system, most of which cannot be seen from a URL or a codebase.

## Facts

- Current edition: ISO/IEC 27001:2022, plus Amendment 1:2024 (climate change considerations in clauses 4.1 and 4.2).
- The transition period from the 2013 edition ended on 31 October 2025. Certificates against 27001:2013 are no longer valid.
- Annex A has 93 controls in four themes: A.5 organisational (37), A.6 people (8), A.7 physical (14), A.8 technological (34).
- Management system clauses 4 to 10 (context, leadership, planning, support, operation, performance evaluation, improvement) are mandatory. Annex A controls are selected through the risk treatment and the Statement of Applicability.

## Manual checklist (needs_manual, SP.CERT.ISO27001)

Ask the owner to confirm, with a document name or link for each:
1. ISMS scope statement (clause 4.3).
2. Information security policy approved by management (5.2).
3. Risk assessment and risk treatment process with results (6.1.2, 6.1.3, 8.2, 8.3).
4. Statement of Applicability (6.1.3 d).
5. Security objectives and how they are measured (6.2, 9.1).
6. Competence and awareness records (7.2, 7.3, A.6.3).
7. Internal audit programme and last report (9.2).
8. Management review minutes (9.3).
9. Nonconformity and corrective action log (10.2).
10. Asset inventory (A.5.9) and acceptable use rules (A.5.10).

Report per theme: checks run, gaps found, items that need a human. No score toward "certification".
