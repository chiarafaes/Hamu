# ISO/IEC 42001 readiness

Readiness only. Relevant when the organisation develops, provides or uses AI systems and named ISO 42001 as a target. Works with the AI Act checks (`aiact.md`) but is a voluntary management system standard, not law.

## Structure

- Clauses 4 to 10: the same management system structure as ISO 27001 (context, leadership, planning, support, operation, evaluation, improvement), with AI-specific items such as AI risk assessment, AI system impact assessment and AI objectives.
- Annex A: 38 controls in nine areas. Annex B gives implementation guidance. Annex C lists AI-related objectives and risk sources. Annex D covers use across domains.

## Manual checklist (needs_manual, SP.CERT.ISO42001)

1. AI policy approved by management.
2. Inventory of AI systems with intended purpose and owner.
3. AI risk assessment and AI system impact assessment per system.
4. Data provenance and quality records for training or retrieval data.
5. Human oversight and escalation for each AI system.
6. Monitoring of AI system performance and incidents after release.
7. Supplier terms for model and API providers (data use, retention, location).
