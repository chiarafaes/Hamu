# POPIA

## When it applies

A responsible party domiciled in South Africa, or one that uses means in South Africa to process personal information (s3). In full effect since 1 July 2021. Authority: Information Regulator.

## Conditions for lawful processing and checks

| Section | Topic (own words) | Check |
|---|---|---|
| 8 | Accountability | SP.PRIVACY.LOCAL-OFFICER |
| 9 to 12 | Processing limitation: minimality, justification (consent or other ground), collected directly where possible | SP.PRIVACY.FORMS, SP.CONSENT.* |
| 13, 14 | Purpose specification, retention limits | SP.PRIVACY.RETENTION |
| 18 | Notification to the data subject when collecting (openness) | SP.PRIVACY.CONTENT |
