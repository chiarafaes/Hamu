# LGPD

## When it applies

Processing in Brazil, processing aimed at offering goods or services to people in Brazil, or of data collected in Brazil (Art. 3). Authority: Autoridade Nacional de Proteção de Dados (ANPD).

## Points the checks use

| Topic | Source | Check |
|---|---|---|
| Ten legal bases, consent must be free, informed and unambiguous, for a specific purpose | Art. 7, 8 | SP.CONSENT.*, SP.PRIVACY.CONTENT |
| Sensitive data | Art. 11 | SP.PRIVACY.SPECIAL |
| Children and adolescents: in their best interest, parental consent for children | Art. 14 | SP.PRIVACY.CHILDREN |
| Data subject rights, including information about sharing and revocation of consent | Art. 18 | SP.PRIVACY.RIGHTS |
| Data protection officer (encarregado), identity and contact published, ideally on the website | Art. 41 | SP.PRIVACY.LOCAL-OFFICER |
| Security measures | Art. 46 | SP.TLS.*, SP.ACCESS.* |
| Incident notification to the ANPD and data subjects within 3 business days (ANPD Resolution 15/2024) | Art. 48 | SP.PRIVACY.BREACH (verify "business days") |
| International transfers: adequacy, ANPD standard contractual clauses (Resolution 19/2024, grace period for existing contracts ended August 2025), or other Art. 33 bases | Art. 33 | SP.TRANSFER.BASIS |

Small-scale processing agents have lighter duties (Resolution 2/2022), for example no mandatory encarregado. Ask about size when Brazil is a target.
