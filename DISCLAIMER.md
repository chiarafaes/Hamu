# Disclaimer

Hamu audit skills produce **readiness checks**. They are not legal advice, not a legal opinion, not a penetration test report and not a certification or attestation (SOC 2, ISO 27001, ISO 42001, HIPAA, PCI DSS or any other).

- Automated and AI-assisted checks miss things. Automated accessibility testing alone finds only part of WCAG issues. Process controls, contracts, policies and training cannot be verified from a URL or a codebase.
- Laws and standards change. Every reference file carries a `last_verified` date. Hamu flags references older than 12 months, but you are responsible for checking the current rules.
- Findings marked "legal review" depend on legal interpretation. Have a qualified lawyer confirm them.
- Only audit targets you own or have written permission to test. Active security tests are off by default and must stay limited to the scope the owner approved.
